---
title: "Resource allowances"
description: "Understand retained workspace limits without a built-in billing provider."
---

Workspace plans and extras define local resource allowances. Operators manage those allowances in Console; enabling a plugin does not automatically grant resources or provision infrastructure.

Billing checkout, payment provider connections, subscription management and invoice summaries have been removed. No replacement billing plugin is currently included. Historical billing records remain retained for existing installations; this change does not cancel external subscriptions or delete provider data.

Review active service usage before reducing allowances. Suspending a service can retain storage and data. Use the relevant service workflow for explicit deletion or handover.

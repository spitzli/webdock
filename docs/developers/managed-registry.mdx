---
title: "Tabularium as a managed app"
description: "Tabularium is operated through Managed Apps; registry configuration stays in your own instance."
---

Tabularium belongs to [Managed Apps](/developers/managed-apps). Webdock operates your dedicated app; you configure the registry in your own Tabularium administration.

The separate `managed-registry` alpha is withdrawn. Do not install it. Its registry-admin API and MCP actions are not part of Managed Apps.

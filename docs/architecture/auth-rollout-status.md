# Auth rollout status — 2026-10-03

Better Auth and the operator workspace are deployed at `auth.webdock.dev` and `admin.webdock.dev`. Webdock, Spitzli and Stall expose `/api/sso/login` and a central-sign-in entry on their existing Payload login pages. The public website frontends and their content remain independent.

The management app is SSO-only; old passwords/JWTs cannot bypass it. Existing websites deliberately remain in temporary legacy-compatible mode. The owner explicitly chose to complete the new central password/MFA enrollment later. Do not enable `WEBDOCK_SSO_ENFORCE=true` across an existing site before its accounts are mapped and ready. Stall still has an existing non-operator account that needs its own onboarding.

Initial operator credentials are in the ignored local `apps/auth/.env.bootstrap`; no real recovery codes were generated by the agent. The real operator must change the bootstrap password and enroll MFA personally. No production email was sent during verification.

Verification completed: real local password-change/MFA/recovery/OIDC flows; organization/project isolation; immediate ban/membership/grant/session revocation; sibling-origin CSRF rejection; production __Host cookie attributes; native-Payload authentication rejection under enforcement; all app builds; live discovery, unauthenticated denial, SSO redirects and public website responses. Authenticated live acceptance with the real operator awaits their enrollment.

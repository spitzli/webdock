---
title: "Get started"
description: "Join a Webdock workspace and find the resources assigned to you."
---

You need a workspace invitation or an existing Webdock account with workspace access. Ask your workspace administrator if you have neither.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">Open your invitation and follow the central sign-in or account setup flow. Complete email verification and accept the invitation. A pending invitation does not grant access.</Step>
  <Step title="Secure your account">Complete the security setup requested by Webdock. Manage your password, passkeys and multi-factor authentication at [My account](https://auth.webdock.dev/account).</Step>
  <Step title="Open Console">Visit [console.webdock.dev](https://console.webdock.dev). Choose your workspace if you have several. Your role and enabled services determine the sections you see.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a resource">Open an assigned website to work on its content, or your workspace's hosting or Mail area. Creating a project record does not automatically deploy an application or provision a CMS.</Step>
</Steps>
